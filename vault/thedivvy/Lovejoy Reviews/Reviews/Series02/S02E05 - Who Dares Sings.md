## Research — verified before watching

- First shown Sunday 3 February 1991, BBC1, 20:05, 55 minutes (TVmaze).
- Written by Douglas Watkinson. Directed by John Woods. Both new to the site.
- Premise: Lovejoy and Jane are hired to fit out a smart new restaurant for
  Major Linden Walker, an old army friend of Tinker's. With help from a former
  prison officer Lovejoy delivers the goods, then finds he has been gulled.
- **Tinker gets a storyline.** He and Linden have a cabaret act, Crime and
  Punishment, making its first and last appearance. After no Tinker at all in
  The Italian Venus and a cellar full of drink in Montezuma's Revenge, this is
  the most he has had to do in Series Two.
- The title puns on the SAS motto, Who Dares Wins, which the army thread sets
  up. Note the title is punctuated "Who Dares, Sings" by TVmaze and TMDB, and
  "Who Dares Sings" by Wikipedia. Slug drops the comma either way.

## Guest notes

- **John Wells (1936-1998)** — guest star. Satirist, actor and writer. Co-wrote
  Private Eye's "Mrs Wilson's Diary", then originated the "Dear Bill" letters
  with Richard Ingrams from 1979, purporting to come from Denis Thatcher. Built
  it into the West End farce Anyone for Denis? (1981), playing Denis himself
  opposite Angela Thorne. Two Bond films: Fordyce in the 1967 Casino Royale,
  and Denis Thatcher in For Your Eyes Only.
- **Peter Armitage (1939-2018)** — Bill Webster in Coronation Street across
  three spells (1984-85, 1995-97, 2006-11), having already played a different
  character there in 1977. Before acting: engineering apprentice, then the
  Merchant Navy. In 1980, after a season at the National, he toured Australia
  13,000 miles by motorbike, busking with a guitar. Good unnecessary detail.
- **Eleanor Summerfield** — veteran of British film and television from the
  forties onward.
- **Robert Austin plays a character called Bruce Wayne.** No explanation
  available. Batman had been in cinemas eighteen months earlier (June 1989),
  so it may not be innocent.
