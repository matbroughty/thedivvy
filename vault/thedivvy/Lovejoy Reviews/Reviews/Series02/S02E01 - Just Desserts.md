## Production notes

- **Director:** Don Leaver
- **Written by:** Dick Clement and Ian La Frenais — an original script, not adapted from a Jonathan Gash novel (Gash keeps a series-wide "characters" credit). La Frenais adapted *Lovejoy* for television in the first place.
- **Production companies:** BBC, Tamariska, WitzEnd Productions (WitzEnd being Clement and La Frenais's own outfit).
- **The five-year gap** was a financing disagreement between the executive producer and the BBC, resolved in 1989 — not a cancellation.
- **Locations:** not documented per-episode. The production deliberately kept locations out of the episode credits, so no reliable source lists what was used for this one specifically. Series-wide it's South Suffolk / North Essex, based around Long Melford, with Belchamp Hall as Felsham Hall. Recurring spots: Lavenham, Finchingfield, Halstead, Coggeshall, Kersey, Walberswick. NB the "Lovejoy's workshop in the grounds of Belchamp Hall" detail applies to the last two series, not this one.
- **Angle worth using:** Clement and La Frenais also wrote *Porridge*. Series Two therefore opens with a prison story written by the men who wrote the definitive British prison sitcom — one week after a Series One finale whose guest star was Fulton Mackay.
