# Title

Series Two — The Divvy Verdict

# Subtitle

Eleven episodes, a higher floor and a lower ceiling. Series Two is better than Series One without ever being as good.

# Main Review

## The argument

Here is the thing I did not expect to find when I added the scores up.

Series Two is running at an average of 4.5. Series One finished on 4.22. On that basis Series Two is the better run, and if you had asked me halfway through I would have said so without hesitating — I have been saying so, repeatedly, in the reviews themselves.

But look at where the two series actually differ.

Series One has **four** fives: *The Judas Pair*, *The Sting*, *Friends, Romans and Enemies* and *The Firefly Cage*. Series Two, six episodes in, has two.

What Series Two has not done is produce a dud. Its worst episode so far is a 4. Series One's average is dragged down by *Death in Venice* at 2.5 and *To Sleep No More* at 3 — one of them a two-part holiday to Venice that mislaid the programme entirely, the other simply thin.

So the sentence I keep reaching for — Series Two is a cracker — turns out to mean something more specific than I meant by it. **The ceiling is lower. The floor is much higher.** Series One could produce the best fifty minutes of the whole thing and then wander off to Italy for a fortnight. Series Two has yet to make anything I would skip.

Which is either a compliment or not, depending on what you want from a programme. Consistency is the thing you notice when it is gone.

## What is driving it

A few things, and they are all the same thing really: this run knows what it is.

**It stays put.** Suffolk and Essex, pubs, cottages, auction rooms, a market square. The one episode that reaches — <Ep>The Italian Venus</Ep>, with its two-million-pound bronze and its baronet — is the joint-lowest scored of the six, and the two that go smallest, *Bin Diving* and *Who Dares Sings*, are the two I liked most. There is a lesson in that which the programme itself appears to have learned faster than I did.

**The crew is the show.** Every score in this series turns out, when I read the reviews back, to be a judgement about who was in it. *Bin Diving* got a five despite having no Tinker and I said at the time I had no defence for that. *The Italian Venus* got docked a star for the same absence a fortnight earlier. *Montezuma's Revenge* got half a star back purely because everybody was present. *Who Dares Sings* was a five until a costume decision. I appear to be scoring the cast list.

**Gimbert is gone and it shows.** Charlie Gimbert is in Series One and then not seen again until Series Four. Two entire series without him. The reviews keep mentioning it, which is usually a sign that something load-bearing has been taken out.

## The ranking so far

*Six of eleven. This will move — five episodes to come, including a
feature-length finale with Linda Gray in it, which could land anywhere.
Renumber as they land; the finished list will be a top eleven.*

**6. The Italian Venus** — [[S02E02 - The Italian Venus]] · 4/5
The first proper sting of the nineties run, and a good one — Ian McNeice being force-fed a cake he never ordered, and a two-million-pound Venus standing in the drawing room with a bulb in its head all along. Docked a star for having no Tinker in it, a rule I then broke a fortnight later and have never satisfactorily explained.

**5. One Born Every Minute** — [[S02E06 - One Born Every Minute]] · 4/5
More fun than its position suggests, and Tom Wilkinson six years before anybody knew his face. Lovejoy and Tinker alone together for a whole hour, which turns out to be a good combination. What holds it back is what it finds charming — a man who picks out lonely women, paints their house for two hours and takes £200 and rather more besides.

**4. Montezuma's Revenge** — [[S02E04 - Montezuma's Revenge]] · 4.5/5
Complete nonsense and I will defend all of it. Three civilisations in one episode, a gold raft that looks like tat, a blowpipe in Suffolk, and a motive that turns out to be a drug debt. Held up by the whole crew being back and by Tinker getting the best speech in the series.

**3. Who Dares Sings** — [[S02E05 - Who Dares Sings]] · 4.5/5
A £300 piano propped up on a 1532 Chaucer, a doctored set of silver, and the whole gang given something to do. It was a five until somebody turned up to the party dressed as Hitler, and the missing half is entirely Adolf's fault.

**2. Just Desserts** — [[S02E01 - Just Desserts]] · 5/5
Five years away and the programme walks back in as though it had never gone. Lovejoy out of prison with a score to settle, Miriam arriving, an antique condom joke inside ten minutes. A confident return that stays almost entirely in Suffolk, having plainly learned something from Venice.

**1. Bin Diving** — [[S02E03 - Bin Diving]] · 5/5
A rubbish round in the rain, a widow with a leaking roof, and Warren Clarke running a parallel economy out of other people's bins. Nobody leaves Suffolk and nobody tries to make Lovejoy into anything he isn't. Five out of five with no Tinker in it, which I still cannot justify and still would not change.

## Notes for finishing this

- **Do not publish until all eleven are reviewed.** The `ranking` frontmatter
  drives ItemList JSON-LD, and the SEO title pattern promises "ranked, worst
  to best". A six-of-eleven list keeps neither promise and would need
  rewriting five times.
- When it does go live it wants `seoTitle: "Lovejoy Series 2 episodes ranked,
  worst to best"` and `heading: "Lovejoy Series Two, ranked"`, matching the
  Series One overview.
- The route registers itself. Dropping `02-overview.mdx` into
  `src/content/series/` is all that is needed — `scripts/lib/routes.mjs`
  derives `/series/2/overview` from the filename.
- The `<Ep>` shortcode above is currently written without a slug as a
  placeholder. Fill in `series-2-episode-2-the-italian-venus` when porting.
- Running average and spread, recalculated on each publish:
  Series 1 — 4.22, spread 2.5–5.0, four fives.
  Series 2 — 4.50 over six, spread 4.0–5.0, two fives.
- If the floor/ceiling argument survives the remaining five episodes it is the
  spine of the piece. If Series Two produces a 2.5 in the back half, the whole
  thing needs rethinking — which is a decent reason not to publish early.
